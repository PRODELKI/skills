### Scope and limitations

- These are **source SKILL.md files**, not complete mirrors of all dependencies, referenced documents, scripts and assets in their upstream repositories. For full functionality, use the original repos and follow their setup steps.
- The original **Screenwriting Skills** project has 26 skills. The two core modules above are a curated selection. The complete set remains available at its [source](https://github.com/jtydhr88/screenwriting-skills/tree/main/plugins/screenwriting/skills).
- `Storytelling` expects an Obsidian vault; it is not a turnkey standalone film generator.
- The fal Cinematography adaptation provides shot plans and review standards; it does not itself run models, and no video generation has been tested here.

### Suggested sequence for a family Christmas short

Story Idea Generator → Cliché Transcendence → Premise Workshop → Character Conflict → Story Structure → Cinematography → test each shot → revise.

Prefer a distinctive conflict, character decisions and visually understandable stakes. Do not add a magical object or a sentimental speech unless it has a precise story function.
